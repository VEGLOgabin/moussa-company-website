About Us

A local dream transformed into an innovative food solution.

Our Mission

To provide the Beninese and African population with healthy, accessible, and gluten-free food products, while valorizing cassava through modern and sustainable processing methods. We believe in food that nourishes the body while respecting the earth.

Our Vision

To become a major player in food innovation in Benin, combining tradition, nutrition, and local development to build a healthier future. We aspire to redefine the standards of natural food in Africa.

Our Story

Founded by Moussa DARI, ECOFEED was born from a simple observation: many people suffering from gluten intolerance did not have access to healthy, local, and affordable products. Leveraging the abundance of cassava in our region, we undertook to transform this traditional root into innovative food alternatives.

From the initial idea to our current products, every step has been guided by our commitment to quality, authenticity, and positive social impact.

Our commitment goes beyond food: we actively support local agriculture, create sustainable jobs in our communities, and raise awareness among the population about the benefits of eating healthy and local. Every ECOFEED product represents a step towards a more sustainable future.

Our Core Values

Health & Wellness

We create nutritious, balanced, and beneficial products for everyone. Our customers' health is our absolute priority.

Respect for Traditions

Inspired by our roots and local know-how, we modernize traditional techniques without losing their essence.

Continuous Innovation

We constantly develop modern solutions for quality food, pushing the boundaries of the agri-food industry.

Proximity & Community

We cultivate a direct relationship with our consumers and producers, creating a fair and transparent value chain.
